# Bounded producer routing cache

`owned_route_cache` copies the frozen `owned_grouped_inline` trainer. The only training change is a same-binary producer-side route cache, controlled by `--route-cache-slots`. Zero slots bypasses the cache. Nonzero values must be a power of two from 2 through 65,536; the value counts **total slots**, with two ways per set. The intended first experiment is 4,096 slots per producer.

Every planning producer owns one cache during its job. A slot contains a nonzero pair key, counted `Delta`, the tail of its grouped birth chain, and the key owner's index. The slot is 32 bytes. Pair key zero denotes an empty slot; valid routed keys have nonzero token IDs. A hit changes only the local slot and, for a birth, appends one eight-byte `BirthNode` to that producer's existing owner-specific birth vector. A miss may evict one of the two slots in its set. The eviction merges checked weight and occurrence counts into the existing owner route map; if it owns a birth segment, its tail is linked to the map's previous head and its head replaces that head. This preserves every node across repeated evictions of one key. The worker flushes all remaining slots before publishing its route output. There is no corpus write until all planning producers finish and flush.

The cache avoids `HashMap` lookup on a hit, but still computes a cheap pair mix and compares up to two keys. It can lose badly under collisions or when initialization and final flush dominate a narrow batch. Every producer initializes and finally scans all its slots in every batch: this adds `O(batch_rounds × workers × slots)` work even when few route events occur. It neither copies the corpus nor removes the eight-byte per-birth temporary node. The fixed allocation is `slots × 32` bytes per producer, plus a small cache object; 4,096 slots cost 128 KiB per producer, or 8 MiB for 64 producers. We report cumulative initialized slots/bytes, the maximum per-batch sum of initialized slot payload (an upper bound on simultaneous live slots), hits, misses, and flushes. Allocator overhead, owner maps, birth vectors, and selected postings remain outside the slot-payload figure. No automatic cache-size tuning is used in this version. Worker count is checked to fit the cached `u32` owner field before pool creation.

AA still uses its exact parity planning path. Its routing calls use the same cache, but cache entries do not participate in AA selection or overlap decisions. Both cache modes must match the complete reference rule `(pair, frequency, order)` trace and final tokens, including weighted pieces, adjacent selected rules, long tokens, and AA runs. The process-CPU timer follows the native CLI convention and includes the entire checked training call.
